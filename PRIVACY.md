# Privacy Policy

**GitHub Comment Translator** is committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection and Usage

- **No Personal Data Collected:** The extension does not collect, log, or track your personal information, browsing history, or identities.
- **Local Storage Only:** Your settings (target language, default translation engine, and custom AI provider configurations including API keys) are stored solely in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`).
- **Direct Requests:** 
  - When using Google Translate, translation requests are sent directly to Google's translation service (`https://translate.googleapis.com/*`).
  - When using custom AI providers, requests are sent directly from the extension background script to the API endpoint you configure. No intermediate servers or third parties receive your requests or API keys.
- **No Analytics or Telemetry:** There are no third-party analytics, tracking scripts, or telemetry embedded in the extension.
- **Data Sharing:** We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer any user data to third parties.
